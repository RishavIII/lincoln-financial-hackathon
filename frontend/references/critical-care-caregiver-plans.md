# Critical Care & Caregiver Support Plans

## Overview
This document summarizes critical care support and caregiver benefit options that may be available during open enrollment. It follows the same format as the insurance package summaries to make comparison easy.

---

## Critical Care Support Plans

### Critical Care Basic
**Monthly Premium:** $150  
**Deductible / Cost-Share:** $1,000 per stay (applies to inpatient services)  
**Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $6,500  

**Coverage:**
- Inpatient ICU care (semi-private room) — covered after deductible
- Short-term step-down unit care
- Critical care physician services
- Emergency transport to nearest appropriate facility (air/ground) — subject to prior authorization when non-emergent
- Palliative consultations (limited visits)

**Best For:** Individuals at moderate risk for acute hospitalizations or those who want basic protection for high-cost inpatient events

---

### Critical Care Plus
**Monthly Premium:** $320  
**Deductible / Cost-Share:** $500 per stay  
**Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $4,000  

**Coverage:**
- Comprehensive ICU and step-down care
- Expanded critical care physician and specialist consults
- ICU-level telemedicine monitoring
- Post-discharge transitional care program (home nurse visits for 14 days)
- Emergency and medically necessary air transport (with fewer restrictions)
- Mental health crisis support following critical events

**Best For:** Those with complex medical histories or household members with chronic conditions that may lead to frequent hospital-level care

---

### Critical Care Premium
**Monthly Premium:** $520  
**Deductible / Cost-Share:** $0 (copays and coinsurance apply)  
**Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $2,000  

**Coverage:**
- Full inpatient critical care coverage with minimal financial barriers
- Private room when medically appropriate
- 24/7 access to critical care nurse hotline
- Comprehensive post-discharge case management and rehabilitation coordination
- Nationwide emergency evacuation and repatriation
- Unlimited palliative and hospice planning consultations

**Best For:** Individuals who want top-tier financial protection and care coordination for serious illness or injury

## Caregiver Support Plans

### Caregiver Stipend (Basic)
**Monthly Cost:** Employer-funded option (no premium to employee)  
**Stipend:** Up to $250/month toward eligible in-home caregiving services

**Coverage / Use:**
- Short-term in-home personal care assistance (bathing, meal prep) — limited hours
- Respite care for family caregivers (up to 30 hours/month)
- Coordination assistance via a dedicated caregiver navigator (limited)

**Best For:** Employees who occasionally need help managing at-home care for an older adult or post-discharge patient

---

### Caregiver Plus (Flexible Benefit)
**Monthly Premium:** $40 (optional payroll deduction)  
**Benefit:** Up to $750/month in reimbursable caregiver expenses (subject to documentation)

**Coverage / Use:**
- In-home skilled and non-skilled caregiving
- Adult day care programs
- Short-term assisted living stays
- Respite care (expanded hours)
- Training resources and counseling for family caregivers

**Best For:** Employees actively providing care to a family member who need sustained support and financial relief

---

### Caregiver Premium (Comprehensive)
**Monthly Premium:** $95  
**Benefit:** Full case management plus up to $1,500/month for approved care services

**Coverage / Use:**
- Dedicated care manager and rapid authorization for services
- Home health aides and licensed nursing when needed
- Long-term care navigation and benefit counseling
- Legal and financial planning consultations (limited sessions)
- Employer-arranged respite and temporary replacement caregiver services

**Best For:** Employees managing heavy caregiving responsibilities who need hands-on support and navigation

---

## Short-Term Crisis Support

- Emergency caregiver placement: immediate temporary placement services to cover the first 7–14 days of a crisis
- Rapid-response home health: priority scheduling for home visits within 24–48 hours after discharge
- Short-term counseling and family therapy for adjustment to acute illness

---

## Flexible Spending & Tax-Advantaged Options

### Dependent Care FSA (Guidance)
**Annual Contribution Limit:** $5,000  

Use pre-tax dollars for eligible caregiving expenses such as adult daycare, in-home care for dependents who qualify, and respite services where permitted by IRS rules.

### Healthcare FSA / HSA (Guidance)
- HSA-eligible plans: may be paired with high-deductible health plans; use for qualified medical expenses including some post-acute care supplies and equipment
- Healthcare FSA: can cover copays, deductibles, and some caregiving supplies; check plan-specific eligible expense lists

---

## Eligibility & Enrollment Notes
- Caregiver stipend and caregiver plans may require proof of need or certification (medical documentation) for continued monthly benefits
- Employer-funded benefits may be subject to limited annual rollover or use-it-or-lose-it rules depending on plan design
- Some caregiver reimbursements are taxable if over certain thresholds — consult plan documents or tax advisor

---

## How to Access Services
- Contact HR benefits portal for initial intake and to request a caregiver navigator
- For critical events, call the 24/7 critical care hotline listed in the benefits portal
- Submit receipts and documentation through the benefits portal or designated vendor for reimbursement
