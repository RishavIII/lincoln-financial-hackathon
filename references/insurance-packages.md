# Open Enrollment - Insurance Package Summaries

## Health Insurance Plans

### Basic Plan
**Monthly Premium:** $125  
**Deductible:** $3,000  
**Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $6,000  
**Primary Care Copay:** $35  
**Specialist Copay:** $70  

**Coverage:**
- Primary care visits
- Emergency care
- Generic prescriptions
- Preventive services (100% covered)

**Best For:** Healthy individuals with minimal healthcare needs

---

### Standard Plan
**Monthly Premium:** $245  
**Deductible:** $1,500  
**Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $4,000  
**Primary Care Copay:** $25  
**Specialist Copay:** $50  

**Coverage:**
- Primary & specialist care
- Emergency care
- Prescriptions (generic & brand name)
- Lab work and diagnostic tests
- X-rays and imaging
- Preventive services (100% covered)

**Best For:** Individuals and families with moderate healthcare needs

---

### Premium Plan
**Monthly Premium:** $380  
**Deductible:** $500  
**Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $2,000  
**Primary Care Copay:** $15  
**Specialist Copay:** $30  

**Coverage:**
- Comprehensive medical care
- Preventive services (100% covered)
- Mental health services
- Physical therapy
- All prescriptions
- Maternity care
- Emergency care worldwide

**Best For:** Those with ongoing medical needs or families planning for major healthcare expenses

---

## Dental Insurance

### Dental Basic
**Monthly Premium:** $25  
**Annual Maximum Benefit:** $1,000  

**Coverage:**
- Preventive care: 100% (cleanings, exams, X-rays)
- Basic procedures: 70% (fillings, extractions)
- Major procedures: 50% (crowns, bridges)

**Best For:** Routine dental maintenance

---

### Dental Plus
**Monthly Premium:** $45  
**Annual Maximum Benefit:** $2,000  

**Coverage:**
- Preventive care: 100%
- Basic procedures: 80%
- Major procedures: 60%
- Orthodontia: 50% (up to $1,500 lifetime max)

**Best For:** Families or those needing extensive dental work

---

## Vision Insurance

### Vision Plan
**Monthly Premium:** $12  

**Coverage:**
- Annual comprehensive eye exam (100% covered)
- Frames: $150 allowance every 12 months
- Lenses: Covered in full every 12 months
- Contact lenses: $150 allowance (in lieu of glasses)
- 20% discount on LASIK

**Best For:** Anyone needing glasses, contacts, or regular eye exams

---

## Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)

### Healthcare FSA
**Annual Contribution Limit:** $3,200  
**Employer Contribution:** $500  

Use pre-tax dollars for:
- Medical copays and deductibles
- Prescriptions
- Dental and vision expenses
- Medical equipment

---

### Dependent Care FSA
**Annual Contribution Limit:** $5,000  

Use pre-tax dollars for:
- Daycare expenses
- After-school programs
- Summer day camps
- Elder care
