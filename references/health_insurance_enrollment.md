# 2025 Health Insurance Open Enrollment

**Enrollment Period:** November 1 - November 15, 2024  
**Coverage Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

---

## Available Health Plans

### Plan 1: PPO Gold Plan
**Monthly Premium:** $185 (Employee Only) | $420 (Employee + Family)

- **Annual Deductible:** $500 individual / $1,000 family
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $3,000 individual / $6,000 family
- **Primary Care Visit:** $25 copay
- **Specialist Visit:** $50 copay
- **Emergency Room:** $250 copay (waived if admitted)
- **Prescription Drugs:**
  - Generic: $10 copay
  - Preferred Brand: $35 copay
  - Non-Preferred Brand: $70 copay
- **Hospital Stay:** 20% coinsurance after deductible
- **Network:** Nationwide PPO network, out-of-network coverage available

**Best for:** Employees who want flexibility and lower out-of-pocket costs when visiting doctors

---

### Plan 2: PPO Silver Plan
**Monthly Premium:** $125 (Employee Only) | $310 (Employee + Family)

- **Annual Deductible:** $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $5,000 individual / $10,000 family
- **Primary Care Visit:** $35 copay
- **Specialist Visit:** $70 copay
- **Emergency Room:** $350 copay (waived if admitted)
- **Prescription Drugs:**
  - Generic: $15 copay
  - Preferred Brand: $50 copay
  - Non-Preferred Brand: $90 copay
- **Hospital Stay:** 30% coinsurance after deductible
- **Network:** Nationwide PPO network, out-of-network coverage available

**Best for:** Employees who want moderate premiums and are generally healthy

---

### Plan 3: HMO Plan
**Monthly Premium:** $95 (Employee Only) | $245 (Employee + Family)

- **Annual Deductible:** $1,000 individual / $2,000 family
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $4,500 individual / $9,000 family
- **Primary Care Visit:** $20 copay
- **Specialist Visit:** $45 copay (referral required)
- **Emergency Room:** $300 copay (waived if admitted)
- **Prescription Drugs:**
  - Generic: $10 copay
  - Preferred Brand: $40 copay
  - Non-Preferred Brand: $80 copay
- **Hospital Stay:** $500 copay per admission
- **Network:** Regional HMO network, no out-of-network coverage except emergencies

**Best for:** Employees who want lower premiums and don't mind choosing a primary care physician

---

### Plan 4: High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA
**Monthly Premium:** $65 (Employee Only) | $180 (Employee + Family)

- **Annual Deductible:** $2,800 individual / $5,600 family
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $5,500 individual / $11,000 family
- **Primary Care Visit:** 100% after deductible (no copay)
- **Specialist Visit:** 100% after deductible (no copay)
- **Emergency Room:** 100% after deductible
- **Prescription Drugs:** 100% after deductible
- **Hospital Stay:** 100% after deductible
- **Network:** Nationwide PPO network
- **HSA Employer Contribution:** $750 individual / $1,500 family (annual)

**Best for:** Healthy employees who want to save on premiums and build tax-free savings

## Additional Benefits Included with All Plans

✓ Preventive care at 100% (no copay, no deductible)  
✓ Annual physical exam  
✓ Immunizations and screenings  
✓ Telehealth visits: $0-$25 copay  
✓ Mental health coverage (same as medical)  
✓ Maternity and newborn care  
✓ Pediatric dental and vision (children under 19)

## Dental Insurance (Optional)

### Dental PPO Plan
**Monthly Premium:** $25 (Employee Only) | $75 (Employee + Family)

- Preventive: 100% covered (cleanings, exams, X-rays)
- Basic: 80% covered after $50 deductible (fillings, extractions)
- Major: 50% covered after $50 deductible (crowns, bridges)
- Annual Maximum: $1,500 per person
- Orthodontia: 50% covered, $1,500 lifetime maximum

---

## Vision Insurance (Optional)

### Vision Plan
**Monthly Premium:** $8 (Employee Only) | $18 (Employee + Family)

- Eye exam: $10 copay (once per year)
- Lenses: $25 copay (once per year)
- Frames: $130 allowance (once every 2 years)
- Contact lenses: $130 allowance (in lieu of glasses)

## How to Enroll

1. Log into the benefits portal at: **benefits.company.com**
2. Review plan details and compare options
3. Select your medical, dental, and vision plans
4. Add dependents if applicable
5. Review and confirm your selections
6. Submit by November 15, 2024

**Questions?** Contact HR at benefits@company.com or call 1-800-555-0123

---

*Note: If you do not make a selection during open enrollment, you will be automatically enrolled in the PPO Silver Plan at the employee-only coverage level.*
